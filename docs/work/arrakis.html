THE PROBLEM

Start with the question.

Student and indie projects can be hard to discover and try, especially for people who do not want to set up a development environment.

THE APPROACH

A decision that matters.

The team proposed a discovery feed that connects a short demonstration to a trial and structured feedback. My assigned scope covers the Next.js frontend and scoring/leaderboard service.
